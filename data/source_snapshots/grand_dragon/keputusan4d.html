About Grand Dragon

An overview of Grand Dragon on Keputusan4D: how often it draws, where it operates, and what its archive covers here.

Grand Dragon is a regionally licensed name followed across Malaysia alongside the domestic operators. It draws every day of the week, so a fresh Grand Dragon result lands here daily. Each draw day carries a single main session, published around 7:10 PM MYT. Results are filed here in the standard 4-digit layout: 1st, 2nd and 3rd, then the Special and Consolation lists. Its headline prize tier, near RM 1.2M, sits in the mid range of the operators listed here. Keputusan4D already holds about 855 of its past draws, so the archive reads as a long continuous record.

Grand Dragon Lotto — known in Chinese as 豪龙 and often shortened to GD Lotto or GD4D — is a four-digit (4D) and six-digit (6D) lottery operated by Dai Long Company Limited. As a Cambodia-licensed operator it runs its draws from its venue in Kandal Province, Cambodia, rather than within Malaysia, yet it has become one of the most widely followed 4D draws among Malaysian players. Alongside its standard 4D game it also runs a 6D draw, and the results for both are published together each evening on its official channel.

What sets Grand Dragon apart from the long-established Malaysian pools is its schedule: it draws every single day of the week, with the result typically published around 7:10 PM Malaysia time. Because the draw runs nightly, there is a fresh Grand Dragon Lotto 4D result to look up every day, including weekends and most public holidays. That daily cadence is the reason a seven-day operator matters to followers: the three big Malaysian pools — Magnum, Sports Toto and Da Ma Cai — generally draw only on Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday (plus occasional special draws), which leaves four nights a week with no new numbers. Grand Dragon fills those gaps, so there is always a current result to read regardless of the day.

Each 4D draw produces a 1st, 2nd and 3rd Prize, followed by a set of Special prize numbers and a set of Consolation prize numbers — the same broad category structure familiar to anyone who follows Malaysian 4D. On the results board you will also see the prizes grouped into two headings, Big (Besar) and Small (Kecil). These are simply labels for how the published board is organised, not any kind of prediction: Big covers all 23 winning numbers across every tier (1st, 2nd and 3rd Prize plus the Special and Consolation numbers), while Small covers only the top three prizes. Knowing the difference helps you read which section of the board a number falls into; the drawn numbers themselves are identical for everyone.
