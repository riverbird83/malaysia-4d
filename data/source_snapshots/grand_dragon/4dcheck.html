About Grand Dragon Lotto

Grand Dragon Lotto (often abbreviated GD Lotto) is a Cambodia-licensed 4-digit numbers game. Unlike the peninsular Malaysian and Singaporean operators, GD Lotto draws every day of the week, which makes it one of the most active 4D feeds in our database — seven draw days a week contribute around twice as many records as a Wed/Sat/Sun operator.

The standard GD Lotto draw produces the same 4-digit prize position structure used regionally: a 1st, 2nd, and 3rd prize plus Special and Consolation prize lists. Results are typically posted to the official feed around 19:30 ICT (Indochina Time, UTC+7).

Games offered

GD Lotto 4D

The flagship and only game on the official GD Lotto feed. Big / Small bet options behave the same way as at Malaysian operators. Permutation (iBox-style) play is supported for players who want every arrangement of their selected digits.

Draw schedule

  • Monday through Sunday — single daily 4D draw, around 19:00 ICT.

Where to play

GD Lotto is licensed in Cambodia. Players who place bets via overseas agents should verify the legal status of doing so in their own jurisdiction before participating — 4dcheck only mirrors public result feeds and is not a betting platform.

A note on cross-operator number matching

4D numbers are drawn independently by each operator. A 4-digit combination that wins on GD Lotto on a given date is not connected to a draw on the same date at Magnum, Da Ma Cai, or Sports Toto. The /history/[number] page on 4dcheck rolls up every recorded win across all 10 operators, but each operator's draw is independent of the others.

Frequently asked questions

Does Grand Dragon Lotto draw every day?
Yes — Grand Dragon is a daily 4D draw, unlike the Wed/Sat/Sun cadence of Magnum, Damacai, and Sports Toto.
Where is Grand Dragon Lotto based?
Cambodia. It's licensed there and offers an online results feed that we mirror.
Is GD Lotto the same as Magnum?
No — they're separate operators in separate countries. Numbers won't match across operators.
Where can I buy Grand Dragon Lotto tickets?
Grand Dragon Lotto tickets are sold at the operator's licensed retail outlets in Cambodia and via the operator's official online channels — see https://www.gdlotto.com/. 4dcheck does not sell tickets and is not affiliated with the operator.
How is Grand Dragon regulated?
Grand Dragon operates under the gaming licence published on its official site. Malaysian peninsular NFOs (Magnum, Da Ma Cai, Sports Toto) are licensed by the Ministry of Finance; East Malaysian operators (Sabah 88, STC, Cash Sweep) are licensed at state level; Singapore Pools is regulated by the GRA; the Cambodian operators (Grand Dragon, Perdana, Lucky Hari Hari) hold commercial Cambodian gaming licences.
Does Grand Dragon tax winnings?
Tax treatment of lottery winnings depends on the operator's home jurisdiction and the player's tax residency. Singapore lottery winnings are not subject to Singapore income tax. Malaysian lottery winnings are generally not taxable for individual recipients. Players should consult a qualified tax adviser for their specific situation — 4dcheck does not provide tax advice.
Where do 4dcheck's Grand Dragon results come from?
Directly from Grand Dragon's official public results feed. Our scheduler runs an ingestion job around each draw window and stores the result row in our database. We do not edit prize numbers — we mirror, normalise, and present them.
