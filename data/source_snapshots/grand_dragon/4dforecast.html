How this Grand Dragon Lotto shortlist is generated

We start from every published Grand Dragon Lotto draw in our archive for the selected window and count how often each digit appears in each position. Recent draws are weighted more heavily than old ones using an exponential decay (a draw from last week counts more than one from last year), and digit-pair and gap patterns are folded in on top. The output is a ranked shortlist of candidate numbers — the same procedure runs for every operator, with no manual picks and no "insider" input.

To keep the method honest, the page also replays it backwards: for each past draw we show the shortlist the model would have produced at the time, next to the actual winning numbers. Matches are highlighted in green, and the footer above totals them into a hit rate. That backtest hit rate is a measure of how the method has performed historically — it is not a probability of winning the next draw.

Read this before you play

Every Grand Dragon Lotto draw is an independent random event. A number that appears frequently in past draws is not more likely to appear again, and a long-absent number is not "due" — this is the gambler's fallacy. Statistical shortlists like this one are a way to explore the historical record, not a way to beat odds of 1 in 10,000. Never spend more than you can afford to lose, and if gambling stops being entertainment for you, please seek help.

Frequently asked questions

When is this page updated?

After each official Grand Dragon Lotto draw (daily). Once the result is in our database, the newest forecast is scored against it and a fresh shortlist is generated for the next draw.

What does the backtest hit rate mean?

It is the share of past draws in the selected window where at least one shortlist number matched a winning number (including permutations). It describes the past only; it does not predict the next draw. Full details are on the accuracy dashboard.

No. 4DForecast is an independent statistics site. We do not sell tickets, accept bets, or have any relationship with Grand Dragon Lotto or any other operator. Always verify winning numbers through the operator's official channels before claiming a prize.
