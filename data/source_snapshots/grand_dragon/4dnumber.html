FAQ

What is the difference between a "Big" bet and a "Small" bet?**

Big Bet: This covers all 23 winning numbers (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 10 Special, and 10 Consolation prizes). It offers a higher chance of winning but with a lower payout for the top prizes.

Small Bet: This only covers the top three prizes (1st, 2nd, and 3rd). Because the risk is higher, the payout for these three spots is significantly larger than a Big bet.

What is a "Box" or "Permutation" bet?

A Box bet allows you to cover all possible arrangements of your chosen 4-digit number. For example, if you bet on "1234," a Box bet covers all 24 permutations (1234, 4321, 2431, etc.). If any of those combinations are drawn, you win.

What does "iBet" mean?

iBet is a cost-effective way to play a Box bet. Instead of paying for 24 separate combinations, you pay as little as $1 (or RM1), and the potential prize is divided by the number of permutations. It’s perfect for players who want to increase their winning chances without a high stake.

What time are the 4D results released?

Malaysia (Magnum, Toto, Da Ma Cai): Draws start around 7:00 PM and are usually fully updated by 7:40 PM (GMT+8), on Wednesdays, Saturdays, and Sundays.

Singapore Pools: Draws are held at 6:30 PM on Wednesdays, Saturdays, and Sundays.

Cambodia (GD Lotto, Perdana, Lucky Hari Hari, 9 Lotto): These are daily draws, usually starting around 7:00 PM.

How is the 6D Jackpot different from 4D?

While 4D requires matching 4 digits (odds of 1 in 10,000), **Dragon Lotto 6D** requires matching 6 digits in exact order (odds of 1 in 1,000,000). The higher difficulty level results in a much larger jackpot, often starting in the hundreds of thousands of dollars.

Are there "Special Draws" in Malaysia?

Yes. In addition to the regular Wednesday, Saturday, and Sunday draws, the Malaysian government occasionally authorises Special Draws on Tuesdays. These are often announced a few weeks in advance and are tracked live on our platform.
