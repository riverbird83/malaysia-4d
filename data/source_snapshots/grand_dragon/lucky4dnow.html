Check a result without mixing draws

Match the operator, game, draw date and draw number first. A 4D result is not interchangeable with a 3D, 5D, 6D or jackpot result. Keep leading zeros: 0315 is a four-digit result, not 315.

First, second and third prizes are separate categories. Special and consolation lists are separate too; finding a number in one list does not make it a first-prize result. Use the operator's rules and published result to settle any claim.

FINAL describes a completed stored draw, not proof that it is the newest draw. OUTDATED means the displayed draw is more than four calendar days old; this is a warning threshold, not an operator schedule. UNAVAILABLE means the result could not be reliably checked.

How these statistics are calculated / 统计方法与局限

Each stored prize position contributes one four-digit number: first, second, third, special or consolation. Exact duplicate records are removed. The same number in different draws still counts each time. Leading zeros are retained.

TOP 3 sorts by frequency; ties use ascending number order.

Periods end at the latest recorded draw, not necessarily today. The earliest recorded date does not prove all intervening draws are present. Missing records can change rankings. These are observed frequencies, not estimates of a future win.

统计使用已收录奖项记录，并非完整官方数据库。次数并非中奖概率；缺失期数与较小样本都会影响排名。每家公司实际覆盖日期以卡片为准。
