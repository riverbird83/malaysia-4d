About Grand Dragon Lotto (GD Lotto)

Grand Dragon Lotto, commonly abbreviated as GD Lotto or referred to as Grand Dragon 4D, is one of Malaysia's two main daily-draw 4D lottery operators (the other being 9 Lotto). It runs 365 draws per year at exactly 7:00 PM Malaysia Time.

GD Lotto is popular with players who prefer daily-draw 4D as opposed to the Wed/Sat/Sun schedule of Magnum, Sports Toto, and Da Ma Cai.

Each GD Lotto draw produces 23 winning 4-digit numbers across three prize tiers:

  • 3 main prizes: 1st, 2nd, 3rd
  • 10 Special prize numbers
  • 10 Consolation prize numbers

Frequently Asked Questions about Grand Dragon Lotto

When does Grand Dragon Lotto draw?

Grand Dragon Lotto (GD Lotto) draws daily at 7:00 PM MYT. Results published online within 5-15 minutes of each draw.

Is GD Lotto a daily draw?

Yes. GD Lotto holds 365 draws per year — every single day at 7:00 PM MYT. Together with 9 Lotto, GD Lotto is one of Malaysia's two daily-draw 4D operators.

How is GD Lotto different from 9 Lotto?

Grand Dragon Lotto and 9 Lotto are separate licensed operators. Both run daily 4D draws but produce independent results — winning numbers are completely different between the two.

What are the GD Lotto prizes for RM1?

Standard Big bet: 1st RM2,500, 2nd RM1,000, 3rd RM500, Special RM200, Consolation RM60. Small bet: 1st RM3,500, 2nd RM2,000, 3rd RM1,000.

Are GD Lotto winnings tax-free in Malaysia?

Yes. All lottery winnings, including GD Lotto prizes, are tax-free for individual players in Malaysia. Legal age 21+ per Betting Act 1953. Read more.
